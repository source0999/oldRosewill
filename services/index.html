Life-changing care 24/7

Rosewill is designed to be a transitional home where the females will be residents  from 6- 18 months. The home will provide 24/7 care for the females to include counseling, transportation for education, job placement and medical attention when needed.

Youth Workshops

Bullying Prevention

Tips to prevent bullying are discussed and the girls are allowed to interact with the speakers and one another to prepare them for the inevitable bully. 

Healthy Relationships

The girls have the opportunity to discuss and engage in dialogue regarding dating violence, acquaintance rape, child sexual abuse and stalking. The sessions end with a summary of what healthy relationships look like. 

Proper Hygiene

Information is shared and discussed concerning routine personal care such as: washing face, brushing teeth, grooming hair, body odor, athlete's foot, and other personal hygiene topics. Information is provided to introduce the girls to the importance of appearances and mannerisms.

Life Skills

We assist the young ladies with life skills to include finances, cooking, household chores, etc...

Fundraisers

To provide funds for additional costs which may arise while caring for females.

24/7 Emergency Placement and Care

Rosewill Loving Arms Covenant House provides a therapeutic, transitional home for girls aged 13-20 affected by sexual abuse and trafficking. Our mission is to create a safe haven, empowering them through tailored care plans and trauma-focused therapy to foster healing and future success.

School Partnerships

Rosewill aims to collaborate with educational institutions to nurture and empower young women, offering essential care and skill-building as they transition into adulthood, fostering resilience and confidence during critical phases of their development.

Community Events

Rosewill aims to positively impact the community by actively participating in events that enhance both the organization and its clients, fostering empowerment for young women as they navigate their healing journey. Together, we strengthen resilience and hope for a brighter future.

Fundraisers

Rosewill thrives on the generosity of community donations, ensuring operational sustainability. Annual fundraisers are vital in supporting our mission to provide a safe haven for young females healing from trauma, allowing them to build successful futures. Your contributions matter!
